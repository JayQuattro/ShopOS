## What

Implements the chosen direction for the work order screen: **workflow tabs + a persistent context bar** — the Tekmetric/AutoLeap tab pattern, plus the always-visible money snapshot our own UI rules require (never hide totals or authorization state).

## Structure

**Context bar** (always visible, replaces the old overview cards):
- Identity: customer (link) · vehicle · location
- Money snapshot: **Estimate** (latest revision) · **Authorized** (aggregate of APPROVED decision lines, computed per load) · **Balance** (invoice total − paid, or "not invoiced")

**Tabs** (default: Jobs & estimate):
1. **Jobs & estimate** — technician/vehicle/stage-key/status cards, customer concern, and the full estimate editor (jobs, options, canned jobs, present)
2. **Parts** — parts ordering + sublet
3. **Work & time** — template, tasks, labor time, quality checks
4. **Inspections & media** — DVI, photos/video, loaner, tracker
5. **Money** — revisions history, invoice, deposits
6. **Activity** — timeline

Tabs are client-side and every tab stays mounted (hidden, not unmounted) — switching is instant and a half-typed task or open estimate editor survives. Same mechanism as the workspace panes.

## Notes

- The SectionNav jump bar is gone — tabs replace it.
- Works unchanged inside the multi-RO workspace (the pane renders the same tabs per workspace tab).
- Estimate panel moved into Jobs & estimate (Tekmetric's insight: the jobs tab IS the estimate); Money keeps the financial record view (revisions, invoice, deposits).
